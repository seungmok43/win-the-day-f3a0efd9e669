There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Reported content is reviewed within 24 hours; violating content is removed and the responsible user is ejected without prior notice.

  2. 성적·선정적 내용, 음란물
  3. 폭력, 자해, 자살을 조장하거나 미화하는 내용

  7. 광고, 스팸, 반복 도배, 자동화 프로그램을 통한 게시
